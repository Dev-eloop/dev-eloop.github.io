NVIDIA Audio2Face in Unity

Audio Face Animator runs the NVIDIA Audio2Face AI model inside Unity. Give it an audio file and it generates synchronised lip-sync and facial expression on ARKit-compatible blendshapes — no manual keyframing, and no internet connection.

Why this exists

NVIDIA Audio2Face produces excellent facial animation from speech, but getting it into Unity has never been straightforward. Unreal has an official LiveLink plugin; Unity does not, and the usual answers involve exporting caches through USD or Maya formats, or writing your own connector to receive streamed blendshape weights.

Audio Face Animator removes that step entirely. The model runs inside your Unity project, and the output lands where you want it — as a Unity AnimationClip you can drop straight into an Animator.

How it works

The plugin analyses a speech recording and drives ARKit-compatible blendshapes on your character, producing synchronised mouth movement and facial expression. Everything is computed locally: CUDA and TensorRT ship with the plugin, so there is nothing to install separately and no connection is required at any point.

What you get

  • Animation from audio filesPoint it at a recording and generate a performance — no keyframing pass.
  • NVIDIA Audio2FaceThe real AI model, running locally inside Unity.
  • Fully offlineNo internet connection required at any stage.
  • ARKit blendshapesWorks with the standard ARKit facial rig your character already uses.
  • Saves AnimationClipsOutput is a normal Unity clip — reuse, edit and blend it like any other.
  • Editor and runtimeGenerate while authoring, or at runtime in a Windows Standalone build.
  • EmotionsPlay a line angrily, or move through several moods inside one clip, with ten emotions the model knows.
  • Eyes and blinkingGaze driven onto your rig's eye bones, an optional look-at target, and involuntary blinking.
  • Blendshapes or bonesOne ARKit shape can drive several blendshapes, or a bone — so a rig whose jaw is a bone works too.

First run

The AI model ships as ONNX. The first time you generate, the plugin compiles a TensorRT engine matched to your specific GPU — roughly a minute on a modern card. That engine is then cached and reused, so it happens once.

In the Editor you can get it out of the way up front via Tools > AudioFaceAnimator > Setup TensorRT Engine, which shows your GPU and a progress bar. The same applies per machine in a shipped build: the first generation call on each player's PC builds and caches an engine, so show your own loading UI while it runs. This is precisely why the model ships as ONNX rather than a prebuilt engine — a prebuilt engine only loads on the GPU architecture it was compiled for.

Requirements

This plugin runs the Audio2Face model on your GPU, so the hardware requirements are specific. Please check them before buying.

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • NVIDIA GPU, compute capability 7.5 or newer — GeForce RTX 20-series or newer, or an equivalent Quadro, RTX A-series or data-center card. AMD and Intel GPUs are not supported.
  • NVIDIA driver 576 or newer. CUDA and TensorRT are bundled; nothing needs installing separately.
  • 6 GB or more free VRAM while generating.
  • About 3 GB of disk space, and roughly the same again while the plugin copies its model files into StreamingAssets.
  • A character model with ARKit blendshapes.
