About DevEloop

DevEloop is a small independent studio publishing on the Unity Asset Store since 2024. We are focused on assets with animations, covering the whole path a clip takes — capture, cleanup, export, or ready-made.

Contact & support

Need help with an asset, hit a bug, or want to talk about a custom Unity tool? Email us — we usually reply within a couple of working days.

dev.eloop@outlook.com

When reporting an issue, please include the asset name and version, your Unity version and render pipeline, and the console output. It gets to a fix much faster.
