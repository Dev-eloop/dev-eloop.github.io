    About DevEloop

    DevEloop is a small independent studio publishing on the Unity Asset Store since 2024. The tools come out of real production problems: getting animation out of Unity and into a DCC app, recording gameplay or mocap straight into an .anim clip, cleaning up finger rotations that retargeting always breaks, and driving a face rig from nothing but an audio file.

    The animation packs are recorded from motion capture and delivered as ready-to-use humanoid clips, so they drop into an existing Animator without extra rigging work.

    • Editor and runtimeMost tools work in play mode and in shipped builds, not just in the Editor.
    • Try before you buyTwo assets are free, including a Lite build of the flagship tools.
    • Kept up to datePackages are maintained across Unity LTS releases and updated regularly.
    • Direct supportQuestions and bug reports go straight to the developer — no ticket queue.
